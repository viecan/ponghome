Phone setup

  1. Corner of the table on your opponent's left side. Tripod about 50 cm behind and 30 cm outside that corner, so a serve can't hit the phone.
  2. Phone upright (portrait), front camera facing you. You can read the results from where you serve.
  3. Height: lens about 50 cm above the table, tilted slightly down toward your end. Higher (up to 80 cm) shows your side of the net better.
  4. Aim along the table toward you so your end line, the net and the long side line near the phone are in view.
  5. Bright, even light. Avoid a window or lamp behind you.
  6. Tap Start. The app finds the table by itself (you see its outline for a moment and "Table found ✓"). If it can not, use Menu → Mark the table by hand.
  7. Serve with a white ball. The serve must bounce once on your side and once on the opponent's side. Results show for 10 seconds or until your next serve.

Speed is the average between the two bounces. Spin type and rev/s are estimated from how the ball curves in the air (topspin dips, backspin floats). No marks on the ball are needed.
