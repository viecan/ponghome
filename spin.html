    Where to put your phone

    Setup steps

    1. Side of the table, in line with the net. The phone looks straight at the net post from 2.5 to 3 m away.
    2. Height: lens at table height or up to 20 cm above (about 76 to 95 cm from the floor). Use a tripod or stand.
    3. Landscape, level, straight on. Phone parallel to the table's long side. Use the normal 1x lens (not 0.5x, it bends straight lines).
    4. Framing: whole table in view, plus about 0.5 m past each end and 1 m above the net. Move back if the ends are cut off.
    5. Light: bright and even. No window or bright lamp behind the table facing the phone.
    6. Background: dark or plain if possible. A dark shirt helps, but the app already ignores big white shapes like your body.
    7. Ball: white 40 mm ball with a thick black line drawn halfway around it.
    8. Calibrate: tap Calibrate, then drag a finger to the left end of the near table edge, lift, then the right end. This sets real speed and the tracking zone.
    9. Slow-mo: same position, camera app in 240 fps slow motion, then upload in the Slow-mo tab for measured spin.

    How results work

    Topspin Backspin Low spin

    Live: spin is estimated from how the ball curves (topspin dips, backspin floats). Phones film too slowly to see the line turn in real time.

    Slow-mo: spin is measured by reading the black line each frame. At 240 fps it can read up to 120 rev/s.

    Side spin can't be seen from the side view.
