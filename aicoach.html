📷 Camera Setup for Accurate Analysis

The AI needs to see your whole body clearly. Follow these 4 rules and detection improves a lot.

1 · Where to put the camera (top view)

  • Behind you, or 45° diagonal behind you on your forehand side.
  • Distance 3 to 4 m, so you have room to move left and right.
  • Do not film from the opponent's end: the table blocks your legs.

2 · Camera height & angle (side view)

  • Phone on a tripod, lens about chest to waist height (1.0 to 1.3 m).
  • Keep it level. Filming from the floor or from above distorts knee and arm angles.
  • Lock the phone: no hand holding, no zooming, no panning.

3 · Framing (9:16 vertical)

  • Your whole body, head to feet, always in frame, even when you step wide.
  • You should fill most of the frame height. Too small = poor detection.
  • Avoid standing directly in line with your opponent from the camera's view.

4 · Light, clothes & settings

  • Bright, even light. Avoid filming toward windows or a bright wall.
  • Wear a shirt and shorts that stand out from the wall and floor.
  • Use 60 fps: fast strokes blur at 30 fps and the wrist gets lost.
  • Clean the lens. Keep other people out of the background.
Quick checklist before you hit record: ☐ tripod behind you or 45° diagonal ☐ 3 to 4 m away ☐ lens at chest height, level ☐ head to feet visible during wide steps ☐ light in front of you ☐ 60 fps.
In the app: if detection still struggles, choose Max accuracy in Analysis Settings and use ✋ Mark me manually to draw a box around yourself.
