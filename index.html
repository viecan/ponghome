What we offer

Coaching tailored to your game,
at your convenience.

We offer personalized table tennis coaching tailored to your skill level, goals, and playing style. Our coaches come to your location, providing one on one training in a comfortable and familiar environment, with a focus on technique, footwork, and match strategy.

With flexible scheduling and one-on-one or small group sessions, we make high quality coaching convenient and accessible for players of all ages and skill levels.

For high profile clients, celebrities, and anyone who values privacy, our private, in home coaching provides a discreet and personalized training experience, allowing you to focus on your game in a comfortable and private environment.

  • Coaches travel to your location
  • One-on-one or small group sessions
  • Flexible scheduling around your week
  • Programs for every level, from fundamentals to elite training
