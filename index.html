What we offer

Personalized coaching, on your schedule

We offer personalized table tennis coaching tailored to your skill level, goals, and playing style. Our coaches come to your location, providing one-on-one training in a comfortable and familiar environment, with a focus on technique, and match strategy.

With flexible scheduling and one-on-one or small group sessions, we make high-quality coaching convenient and accessible for players of all ages and skill levels.

For those who value privacy, including public figures, our private in-home coaching offers a discreet and personalized training experience. Train in a space where you feel comfortable, without the crowds or distractions of a traditional club. Your time, your space, your game, coaching built around you.

  • Coaches travel to your location
  • One-on-one or small group sessions
  • Flexible scheduling around your week
  • Programs for every level, from fundamentals to elite training
