Pong Home x PingPoint: Our Test Kit and First Game on Camera

Pong Home is now testing PingPoint's cloud-based scorekeeping hardware courtside. Here is what came in the test kit, and footage of a real game scored live with the PingPoint button and app.

Pong Home Insights Hub · Equipment · 5 min read
PH
Pong Home Insights Hub · Partnership announcement and hands-on test

Pong Home is stepping onto the table with PingPoint. As part of a new hands on partnership, PingPoint sent Pong Home a test kit to try its cloud based scorekeeping hardware in real games, on our own table, with our own camera rolling. Here is the unboxing, a look at the game we played with PingPoint keeping score, and where this goes from here.

Why We Partnered With PingPoint

Pong Home has already published an independent PingPoint review based on the platform's public features and pricing. This partnership takes that a step further. Instead of only reading about smartwatch scoring, the PingPoint button, and match analytics, we wanted to put the hardware in our hands and score an actual match with it, on camera, for the community.

PingPoint is a cloud based table tennis scorekeeper and analytics platform built around one idea: turn every game into data and every match into progress. Track scores in real time, analyze performance, connect with players nearby, and climb the global leaderboard, all from a smartwatch, a physical PingPoint button, or the mobile app. You can see the full platform at pingpoint.pro.

Unboxing the PingPoint Test Kit

The first step was simple: open the box. PingPoint shipped Pong Home a test kit built around the physical PingPoint button, the small tap sensor that lets players log points without needing a smartwatch. Below is our short unboxing clip showing what came in the kit and our first impressions straight out of the box.

Unboxing the PingPoint test kit

The button itself is compact enough to sit right on the edge of the table or clip onto a wrist, which matches what PingPoint describes on its own site: attach the button to the table, or wear it, and tap to log a point without breaking focus on the rally.

Putting PingPoint to the Test on Court

Unboxing hardware is one thing. Using it mid rally, at match pace, is the real test. We set up the PingPoint button courtside and played a full game with the PingPoint app tracking the score live in the background, updating point by point as the rally count climbed. Here is that game in action.

Game day, powered by PingPoint

First impression: Once the button was paired to the app, scoring stayed out of the way. A tap after each rally kept the score current without either player needing to look away from the table or call out the count by hand.

What This Partnership Means for Pong Home Readers

Pong Home is not paid to say PingPoint is good. What this partnership gives us is early, hands on access to the hardware, so future coverage on the Insights hub can speak from actual use rather than a spec sheet alone. Expect more footage and follow up notes as we log more matches with the kit, including how the button holds up over repeated sessions and how the app's analytics look once there is a real match history behind them.

If you want the full breakdown of PingPoint's features, pricing tiers, and who the platform suits best, our standalone PingPoint review covers that in depth. If you are also comparing equipment while you are at it, our Gear Index and Marketplace are good next stops.

PingPoint at a Glance

For readers new to PingPoint, here is the short version. It is a freemium platform with a free tier covering up to 10 matches a month, plus paid Plus and Pro tiers that unlock unlimited matches, advanced analytics, and doubles scoring. Core features include real time scorekeeping from a smartwatch, the PingPoint button, or the mobile app, along with match analytics, an ELO based rating, leaderboards, and player matching.

Want the full breakdown? Pricing tiers, feature by feature detail, and who each plan suits best are covered in our dedicated PingPoint review.

Frequently Asked Questions

Partnership

What is the partnership between Pong Home and PingPoint?

Pong Home is testing PingPoint's hardware and app in real games and sharing the results with the community. Pong Home received a PingPoint test kit, including the PingPoint button, and has been using it courtside to score matches and record match footage for the Insights hub.

Test Kit

What comes in the PingPoint test kit?

The test kit Pong Home received includes the physical PingPoint button, the small tap sensor that attaches near the table or clips onto a wrist, along with setup materials for pairing it with the PingPoint mobile app to start logging points.

How It Works

How does the PingPoint button score a match?

Each player taps the PingPoint button after winning a rally, and the PingPoint app updates the live score, serve rotation, and game count in real time. The match result is then saved to the player's PingPoint profile for stats and rating history.
