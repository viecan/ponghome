RacquetsUp Expo 2027

Canada's premier racquet sports event returns to Toronto this February. Pong Home is on the court, with an exclusive discount code available on this page for our table tennis community.

Pong Home Insights Hub · Events · 8 min read
PH
Pong Home Insights Hub · Partnership announcement and event guide

Every so often, an event comes along that brings the entire racquet sports world under one roof. RacquetsUp Expo is that event, and this February it lands in Toronto for a weekend built for players, fans, and racquet sport lovers of every level.

Pong Home is proudly partnered with RacquetsUp Expo, one of the largest events for racquet sport lovers in North America. As a table tennis coaching and gear platform built around this sport, we know how rare it is to see tennis, table tennis, pickleball, padel, squash, and badminton share the same weekend, the same city, and the same energy. We are excited to be part of it, and even more excited to see the table tennis community show up in force.

What is RacquetsUp Expo?

RacquetsUp Expo is shaping up to be Canada’s premier racquet sports experience, bringing players, brands, and the racquet sports community together for an action-packed weekend. Built around community, product discovery, and on-court experiences, the Expo lets you connect with fellow players, test the latest gear, watch live demos, learn from top players and coaches, and compete in an exciting mini tournament. It’s more than an expo, it’s a weekend to play, connect, and elevate.

The Expo is held in partnership with Canada’s national governing bodies, including Table Tennis Canada, giving the event a level of credibility and reach that few gear or lifestyle shows can match. Its tagline says it best: Play. Connect. Elevate.

The Enercare Centre is the largest exhibition space in Toronto, which gives the organizer the room to run multiple courts, demo zones, and vendor booths at the same time. If you have ever wished you could try various racket sports, and equipment in a single afternoon, this is exactly that kind of weekend.

Why it matters for table tennis players

Table tennis rarely gets a stage this big. RacquetsUp Expo changes that, bringing players together for a full day of gear, demos, competition, and community.

  • Test the latest gear including blades, rubbers, and rackets before you buy.
  • Watch live on-court demos from experienced and competitive players.
  • Join a mini tournament and put your skills to the test.
  • Connect with coaches, clubs, and players from across the community.
  • Explore the wider racquet sports world, including tennis, pickleball, padel, squash, and badminton.
