RacquetsUp Expo 2027

Canada's premier racquet sports event returns to Toronto this February, and Pong Home is on the court to welcome the table tennis community.

Pong Home Team · Club Culture · 5 min read
PH
Pong Home Team · Partnership announcement and event guide

Every so often, an event comes along that brings the entire racquet sports world under one roof. RacquetsUp Expo is that event, and this February it lands in Toronto for a weekend built for players, fans, and racquet sport lovers of every level.

Pong Home is proudly partnered with RacquetsUp Expo, one of the largest events for racquet sport lovers in North America. As a table tennis coaching and gear platform built around this sport, we know how rare it is to see tennis, table tennis, pickleball, padel, squash, and badminton share the same weekend, the same city, and the same energy. We are excited to be part of it, and even more excited to see the table tennis community show up in force.

What is RacquetsUp Expo?

RacquetsUp Expo bills itself as Canada's premier racquet sports experience, and after one look at the lineup, it is easy to see why. The event is organized around four pillars: community connections, product discovery, health and wellness, and on-court demos. In practice, that means a weekend where you can meet other players, test new gear before you buy it, watch live demonstrations, and pick up tips from people who play at a very high level.

The Expo is running in partnership with Canada's national governing bodies for major racquet sports, giving the event a level of credibility and reach that few gear or lifestyle shows can match. Its tagline says it best: play, connect, elevate.

The Enercare Centre is one of the largest exhibition spaces in Toronto, which gives organizers the room to run multiple courts, demo zones, and vendor booths at the same time. If you have ever wished you could try a dozen different rackets, blades, or rubbers in a single afternoon instead of ordering them one at a time online, this is exactly that kind of weekend.

Why it matters for table tennis players

Table tennis often gets grouped in with the wider racquet sports world, but it rarely gets a stage this big. RacquetsUp Expo changes that. For players in and around Toronto, the event is a chance to:

  • Try blades, rubbers, and rackets in person before committing to a purchase, something our own Gear Index is built to support once you know what you are looking for.
  • Watch on-court demos from experienced and competitive players, picking up small technical details that are hard to learn from video alone.
  • Meet coaches, clubs, and other players face to face, which is exactly the kind of community connection that keeps people playing for years instead of months.
  • See how table tennis fits into the broader health and wellness conversation, alongside tennis, pickleball, padel, squash, and badminton.

A weekend like this does more than sell gear.

It reminds players why they picked up a paddle in the first place. Whether you are a competitive athlete chasing your next tournament or someone who plays casually with friends on the weekend, there is something at RacquetsUp Expo for you.

Pong Home's role in the partnership

Pong Home was built around one goal: helping table tennis players find the right coach, the right gear, and the right community. Partnering with RacquetsUp Expo is a natural extension of that mission. It puts us in the same room as the players, coaches, and brands who make this sport what it is, and it gives us a chance to connect with the community in person rather than only online.

We will be following the Expo closely and sharing more details as the event gets closer, including any table tennis specific programming, coaching connections, and gear highlights worth knowing about.

Save the date. RacquetsUp Expo runs February 19 to 21, 2027 at the Enercare Centre, Exhibition Place, Toronto. Tickets are on sale now at racquetsupexpo.com.

Getting the most out of the weekend

If you are planning to attend, a little preparation goes a long way. Bring a change of clothes if you plan to try any on-court demos, since you may end up playing more than you expect. If you already know your current blade and rubber setup, jot it down before you go so you can compare new options against something familiar rather than guessing from memory. And if you are shopping for a first racket or an upgrade, our Gear Index is a useful starting point for narrowing down what to look for before you get to the show floor.

Play. Connect. Elevate. That is the promise of RacquetsUp Expo, and Pong Home is glad to be part of making it happen for the table tennis community.
