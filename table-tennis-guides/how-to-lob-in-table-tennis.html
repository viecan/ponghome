How to Lob in Table Tennis: Turning Defense Into a Weapon

How to turn a defensive lob from a last resort into a genuine tactical weapon.

Pong Home Team · Technique · 5 min read
PH
Pong Home Team · Coach-written technique guide

When you are out of position against a hard hit ball, the instinct to panic is strong. A well executed lob buys you time, changes the shape of the point, and can even set up a counter-attack of your own.

When to Use a Lob

A lob is most useful when you are pulled well off the table or badly out of position and cannot safely play a controlled shot. Rather than rushing a weak return, a high, deep lob gives you time to recover your stance while forcing your opponent to smash from a distance, which is a harder shot than it looks.

Executing the Lob

  • Get under the ball early, using your legs to drive the shot upward rather than just your arm.
  • Aim for height and depth, a lob that clears the table by several feet and lands deep gives your opponent the least amount of time and angle to work with.
  • Add spin whenever possible. A lob with topspin or sidespin is significantly harder to smash cleanly than a flat, spinless one.

Fishing: The Lower Lob Variation

Not every defensive shot needs to be a towering lob. "Fishing" is a lower, flatter defensive shot played from mid-distance that keeps the ball in play without giving your opponent a huge, obvious target to smash. It is a useful middle ground between a full defensive lob and trying to counter-attack from an unstable position.

Coaching tip: Stay ready after every lob. A high percentage of smashed lobs come back at a similar height and speed, and defenders who relax after lobbing often get caught flat-footed on the next shot.

Turning Defense Into Offense

Good defensive players do not just survive with the lob, they use it to set traps. A well placed, heavily spun lob often draws a rushed or overhit smash, giving the defender a clean opportunity to counter-attack on the next ball.

Frequently Asked Questions

Defensive Shots

Is the lob only a last resort shot?

It is primarily a recovery shot used when you are out of position or facing a shot you cannot otherwise return safely, but skilled defenders also use it deliberately to draw a weaker smash and set up a counter-attack.

Defensive Shots

How do I add spin to a lob?

Brush across or up the ball rather than striking it flat, similar to a loop but with a longer, higher arc. Sidespin and topspin lobs are both common and make the ball harder to smash cleanly.
