Truls Moregardh: The Magician Reviving European Table Tennis

How a Swedish underdog ranked outside the world's top 100 became the first non-Chinese men's singles player in a generation to reach an Olympic final.

Pong Home Insights Hub · Athlete · 8 min read
PH
Pong Home Insights Hub · Coach-written player profile

In a sport long dominated by China, Truls Moregardh has become European table tennis's biggest story in a generation. With a flair for the dramatic, a deep bag of unorthodox shots, and a run to the Olympic final that stunned the sport, the young Swede has revived interest in table tennis across an entire continent. Here is how he plays, how he wins, what he has achieved, and what is on his blade.

Who Is Truls Moregardh

Truls Carl Eric Moregardh was born on February 16, 2002, in Lessebo, Sweden, and grew up in the nearby town of Hovmantorp, where he first picked up a paddle at age five on a makeshift table in the family garage. His older brother Malte, also a table tennis player, later became his coach, and their parents raised both boys around a culture of competitive sport.

Moregardh's rise has been remarkably fast. He was ranked 103rd in the world as recently as March 2021. A stunning run to the final of the 2021 World Table Tennis Championships in Houston, where he entered ranked 77th and became the lowest ranked player ever to reach a World Championship men's singles final, changed the trajectory of his career overnight.

Playing Style: Creative, Deceptive, and Entertaining

Moregardh plays a right handed shakehand grip in the European style, and his game is built on variety and misdirection rather than raw power alone. He is frequently compared to Swedish legend Jan-Ove Waldner for the way he blends craft with unpredictability.

  • Backhand blocking and punching: Like Waldner before him, Moregardh uses his backhand as both a defensive wall and a sudden attacking weapon, changing pace without warning.
  • The chop block: One of his signature techniques, this unconventional shot combines a defensive chop motion with a blocking action, producing awkward, low bouncing returns that disrupt an opponent's rhythm.
  • Mixing aggression with defense: Rather than committing fully to one style, Moregardh moves fluidly between aggressive forehand attacking and more patient, defensive rallying depending on what the point calls for.
  • Shot deception: His preparation and follow through often look similar across very different shots, making it difficult for opponents to read spin and direction before contact.

Tactics: How Moregardh Wins the Big Points

Moregardh's tactical identity is built around unpredictability and mental resilience in long matches.

  • Constantly shifting pace: By alternating between hard attacking shots and softer, spin heavy touches, he prevents opponents from settling into a comfortable rhythm.
  • Using the chop block to reset rallies: When under pressure, Moregardh often reaches for his chop block to neutralize an opponent's attack and force a fresh exchange on his own terms.
  • Thriving in marathon matches: His run to the 2025 WTT Europe Smash title included a seven game win over then world number one Lin Shidong, a reflection of his stamina and composure deep into deciding games.
  • Playing to the crowd: Moregardh visibly feeds off home support and big occasions, often raising his level noticeably in front of large, engaged crowds.
Coaching note: Club players can borrow Moregardh's core idea without his flair: varying pace and spin, not just power, is often what actually breaks an opponent's rhythm in a rally.

Career Timeline and Major Achievements

Moregardh's résumé has grown quickly since his 2021 breakthrough. Key milestones include:

  • 2021: Reached the final of the World Table Tennis Championships in Houston as the lowest ranked finalist in the tournament's history, winning a silver medal.
  • 2023: Won the European Championships team title with Sweden.
  • 2024 Paris Olympics: Won a silver medal in men's singles, becoming the first non-Chinese player to reach an Olympic singles final in over two decades, plus a silver medal with Sweden's men's team.
  • 2025: Won the WTT Europe Smash in Sweden, becoming the first non-Chinese player to win a WTT Grand Smash singles title, then added the WTT Champions Montpellier title later the same year.
  • 2025 World Championships: Won a bronze medal in men's singles.
  • March 2026: Reached a career high world ranking of number 2 in men's singles.

Why People Love Truls Moregardh

Moregardh's popularity has grown well beyond table tennis's traditional audience, and a few things explain why:

  • A genuine underdog story: His climb from a ranking outside the world's top 100 to an Olympic final in just a few years feels like the fairytale he himself has described it as.
  • Reviving European table tennis: His success has been credited with a surge of public interest in the sport across Sweden, including new broadcast deals and packed arenas for WTT events on home soil.
  • An entertaining, watchable style: His flair for dramatic shots and creative shot selection make his matches must watch even for casual fans unfamiliar with the sport's finer details.
  • Relatable humility: Despite his rapid rise, Moregardh is widely seen as approachable and grounded, co writing a book with his father on mindset and high performance that reflects his thoughtful approach to the game.

Equipment: What Is On Moregardh's Racket

Truls Moregardh plays with his own signature blade, the Stiga Cybershape Carbon CWT Truls Edition, developed with longtime sponsor Stiga around his need for both control on defensive shots and pop on sudden attacking strokes. The Cybershape design, with its distinctive handle geometry, is built to support the variety that defines his game.

Frequently Asked Questions

Player Spotlight

Why is Truls Moregardh compared to Jan-Ove Waldner?

Moregardh shares Waldner's deceptive, creative style, especially his backhand blocking, backhand punching, and unpredictable shot selection, which is why Swedish fans see him as the natural heir to their sport's most legendary player.

Career

What is Truls Moregardh's biggest career achievement?

His silver medal in men's singles at the 2024 Paris Olympics stands as his signature achievement, making him the first non-Chinese player to reach an Olympic singles final in over two decades. He backed it up in 2025 by becoming the first non-Chinese player to win a WTT Grand Smash singles title.

Equipment

What equipment does Truls Moregardh use?

Moregardh plays with his own signature blade, the Stiga Cybershape Carbon CWT Truls Edition, paired with Stiga DNA Platinum XH rubber on both his forehand and backhand.

Playing Style

What makes Truls Moregardh's playing style unique?

Moregardh blends aggressive forehand attacking with unconventional defensive tools like the chop block, mixing pace, spin, and placement in ways that make his shot selection difficult to predict.
