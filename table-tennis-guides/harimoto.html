Tomokazu Harimoto: Speed, Fearlessness, and the New Wave

How the Japanese prodigy became the youngest superstar in table tennis, with his blazing speed, signature scream, and a game built for the modern era.

Pong Home Insights Hub · Athlete · 7 min read
PH
Pong Home Insights Hub · Coach-written player profile

Tomokazu Harimoto is the face of a new generation. Bursting onto the international scene as a teenager, he has redefined what is possible for a young player, beating established Chinese superstars and becoming the youngest winner of the ITTF World Tour Grand Finals. This is a deep dive into his ultra-aggressive style, his biggest triumphs, and the equipment that fuels his lightning-fast game.

Who Is Tomokazu Harimoto

Born on June 27, 2003, in Sendai, Japan, Tomokazu Harimoto is a table tennis prodigy. Born to a Chinese-born table tennis player mother and a Japanese father, he started playing at age two and quickly showed extraordinary talent. His family moved to Japan, and he was soon training at a professional level. He became the youngest player ever to win the ITTF World Tour Grand Finals at just 15, a feat that instantly made him a global sensation.

Harimoto's rise has been meteoric. He consistently features in the top 5 of the ITTF world rankings and has become the most prominent non-Chinese player on the men's circuit. His rivalry with the Chinese national team, particularly players like Fan Zhendong and Wang Chuqin, has defined the sport's modern narrative, making him a figure of immense interest for fans worldwide.

Playing Style: Speed, Aggression, and the Scream

Harimoto is a right-handed shakehand player. His style is built on one thing: speed. He takes the ball incredibly early, often on the rise, to rush his opponent and eliminate their preparation time. This fearless, all-out attack approach is his trademark.

  • Impeccable timing: Harimoto's ability to step in and take the ball early with his backhand and forehand drives is exceptional. This gives his shots a unique sharpness and low trajectory.
  • Powerful backhand drive: His backhand is arguably his most devastating weapon. He can generate blistering speed with a short, compact swing, making it a constant threat for winners down the line and cross-court.
  • Fearless third-ball attack: Like many modern greats, Harimoto looks to attack the third ball with immense power. He uses his serve to set up a quick, decisive finish.
  • The psychological factor: His loud screams are a famous part of his game. They are both a personal release of energy and a tactic to unnerve his opponents and assert dominance.

Tactics: How Harimoto Wins Points

Harimoto's tactical approach is a relentless pursuit of the initiative. He doesn't wait, he attacks.

  • Serve & drive: He uses a variety of serves, but his goal is always the same: to get a weak or predictable return that he can attack with a powerful drive from his forehand or backhand.
  • Backhand dominance: He often targets his opponent's middle or wide forehand with his backhand to open up the table. He then follows up by stepping around to finish with his forehand.
  • Intense footwork: His speed around the table is elite. He uses his movement to position himself for high percentage, high-power shots, avoiding being caught out of position.
  • Mental toughness: Despite his youth, Harimoto has shown incredible mental resilience, often pulling off comebacks and winning crucial deuce points in high-pressure matches.
Coaching note: Amateur players can learn from Harimoto's ability to take the ball early. Focus on your footwork to get into position quickly, and try to play more shots off the bounce rather than waiting for the ball to fall into your comfort zone.

Career Timeline and Major Achievements

Harimoto's list of achievements is remarkable for a player of his age:

  • 2018: Became the youngest-ever winner of the ITTF World Tour Grand Finals at age 15, defeating a field of top players.
  • 2019: Won the ITTF World Tour Czech Open, and became the youngest player to reach the semi-finals of the World Championships.
  • 2022: Reached a career-high world ranking of No. 2, solidifying his position as the top non-Chinese player.
  • 2023: Captured the WTT Championship in Budapest, beating several top Chinese players on his way to the title.
  • Has won multiple ITTF World Tour and WTT Series singles titles, alongside team medals for Japan.

His rapid ascension and consistent performances against the Chinese elite have made him the most exciting and significant non-Chinese male player of his generation.

Why People Love Harimoto

Few players evoke as strong a reaction as Tomokazu Harimoto. His popularity, especially among younger fans, is undeniable:

  • Fearless youth: He is the poster child for a new era of fearless young players who are not intimidated by the legacy of the Chinese national team.
  • Expressive emotion: He wears his heart on his sleeve. His passion, energy, and loud screams are a direct contrast to the often subdued nature of many top players, making him compelling to watch.
  • Breaking the status quo: His success has helped to internationalize the sport's top tier, providing a genuine rival to the established order.
  • Relatability: His story of leaving his home country to pursue a dream in Japan, combined with his youthful exuberance, has made him a hero to many aspiring young players around the world.

Equipment: What Is On Harimoto's Racket

Tomokazu Harimoto uses a setup tailor-made to enhance his aggressive, high-speed style. He plays with the Butterfly Harimoto Super ZLC, his own signature blade. This blade uses a Super Zylon Carbon (SZLC) material, offering a unique blend of a large sweet spot and superb ball feedback, perfect for generating high ball speed while maintaining control. It's known for being a fast blade without sacrificing the feeling needed for good touch.

For rubbers, Harimoto uses Butterfly Dignics 09C on both his forehand and backhand. This is a hybrid rubber that combines a tacky topsheet with a springy sponge. The tackiness provides excellent spin on serves and loops, while the springy sponge delivers the explosive speed required for Harimoto's drives, especially his devastating backhand. This setup allows him to produce the high level of spin and speed that has become his trademark, giving him the confidence to take the ball as early as he does.
