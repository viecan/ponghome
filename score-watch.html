Table Tennis Scoreboard Remote for Apple Watch

Pong Home's scoreboard remote is a tap to score tool built for Apple Watch. Choose best of 3, best of 5, or best of 7 before the match starts. Each game is played to 11 points and must be won by at least 2 points. Your score sits on the left and your opponent's score sits on the right, with a large Pong Home brand header centred at the top, a reset button in the top left, and an undo button in the top right.

Match format and scoring rules

Tap a player's side to add one point for them. A game ends the moment a player reaches 11 points with a 2 point lead, and ties beyond 10 to 10 continue until that 2 point margin appears. Game wins accumulate toward the chosen best of format until one player clinches the match.
